# Social card v1 — provenance

Generated 20 July 2026 with OpenAI image generation at the owner’s explicit request. The four Visual Family v2 PNG masters were supplied as style and material references.

## Final prompt

Create one hauntingly beautiful, playful editorial launch card for Here’s The Thing. A small handmade paper mechanism becomes a coherent web of four connected decisions: one slide, a Film & TV deck plan, the next move, and a pitch dictionary. Let a hot-pink thread connect four distinct paper moments without becoming a flowchart. Use warm off-white paper, graphite construction marks, restrained cobalt tape, tiny acid-yellow accents, believable soft studio light, and strong thumbnail silhouette. Weight the artwork to the right and keep the left calm. No text, logo, letters, UI, device frame, glossy 3D, stock people, dogs, watermark, region-specific ornament, or SVG-like shapes.
