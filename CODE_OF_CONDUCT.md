# Code of conduct

This project is for people making things under uncertainty. The community should reduce unnecessary fear, not reproduce the ugliest parts of the industries around us.

## Expected

- Critique work specifically; treat people as more than their output.
- Make room for people historically pushed out of film, technology, design, and capital.
- Respect names, pronouns, boundaries, access needs, and different levels of formal experience.
- Credit labour and references. Do not quietly absorb somebody else’s work into your authority.
- Challenge harmful assumptions and power imbalances without turning disagreement into spectacle.
- Accept correction, repair what you can, and move on without demanding emotional caretaking from the person affected.

## Not welcome

- harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, sexualised attention, or intimidation;
- racism, casteism, sexism, misogyny, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, xenophobia, class contempt, or religious bigotry;
- jokes or “debate” that target marginalised people;
- professional retaliation, coercive networking, or exploiting unpaid labour;
- publishing private material or contact information without consent;
- bad-faith disruption, spam, or repeatedly exhausting maintainers after a boundary is clear.

## Reporting

For a security issue, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact route on [pitch.dog](https://pitch.dog/) rather than opening a public issue. Include only what is needed to understand the incident.

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject contributions and participation that violate this policy. Enforcement should protect affected people, preserve privacy, and avoid punitive theatre.
