# Design: useful, then unforgettable

The interface has a point of view: a deck is a physical sequence of decisions, not a beige form with a gradient on top.

## The family resemblance

All four tools share:

- warm paper, ink, cobalt, hot pink, and acid chartreuse;
- blunt sans-serif statements beside editorial serif explanation;
- a continuous thread as navigation, not decoration;
- physical 16:9 cards, seams, braces, locators, and typographic sorts;
- animated grain and a restrained WebGL field;
- motion that clarifies focus and collapses when reduced motion is requested.

They do not share identical layouts. Slide Check is a workbench. Deck Bones is a sequence. Next Move is a locator. The dictionary is a room of language.

## Interaction rules

1. **The active decision owns the screen.** Previous and future work recede.
2. **A choice explains itself.** Labels alone are rarely enough.
3. **Blank input is exceptional.** Slide copy is typed because the browser actually measures it. Deck strategy is chosen because the software cannot honestly interpret free prose.
4. **Depth is consensual.** The quick Deck Bones ride is useful. The deeper route is invited, never sprung.
5. **Progress is visible.** Every guided journey shows position and remaining work.
6. **Results lead with the answer.** Evidence and caveats remain available without sitting on the user’s chest.
7. **Delight survives without motion.** No essential meaning lives only in WebGL, hover, colour, or animation.

## Visual assets

The source PNGs and complete prompts live in `website/assets-source/`. Runtime WebPs live in `website/public/assets/visual-family-v2/`.

Do not redraw the logo or the visual family as invented SVGs. Do not turn the tactile artwork into generic icons. Preserve aspect ratio, crop with intent, and let artwork merge into its colour field rather than float in a little white rectangle.

## Anti-slop checklist

- Does this screen make the next action obvious without a tour?
- Is the copy specific to deck-making?
- Is anything pretending to be intelligent when it is only storing text?
- Does a flourish express the mechanism, or merely advertise that animation exists?
- Could the layout belong to any productivity SaaS? If yes, it is not finished.
- Is the mobile journey a composition, not a desktop page squeezed through a letterbox?
- Can keyboard, touch, reduced-motion, and high-contrast users finish the same job?

Soul is not an extra animation. It is care made visible at every decision boundary.
