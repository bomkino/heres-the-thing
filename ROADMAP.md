# Roadmap

The roadmap is deliberately about evidence, not feature confetti.

## Strong next work

- Validate Deck Bones routes with working Film & TV producers, writers, directors, commissioners, labs, and funders in more than one market.
- Calibrate Slide Check reading and fit defaults with real rooms, fonts, languages, and accessibility settings.
- Expand Pitch Dictionary from observed confusion, not SEO volume.
- Add documented translation architecture without pretending one English editorial band transfers cleanly to every language.
- Publish accessibility and performance audits with reproducible browser/version details.
- Add import/export only when the privacy boundary remains legible.

## Not planned by default

- accounts, saved-cloud projects, or analytics;
- runtime generative AI;
- “AI scores” for creative quality;
- a generic slide designer;
- forced funnels joining all four tools;
- gamification that makes people finish questions instead of solving the deck.

Open an issue with a concrete user and decision if one of these boundaries should change.
